Integrated Competitive Programs

Specialized Competitive Wings

Comprehensive preparation for national entrance examinations conducted alongside Telangana SSC Board curriculum in our designated campus wings.

Air-Conditioned Campus Grades 6th to 10th

Integrated IIT-JEE & NEET Foundation Academy

Designed for ambitious high school students seeking early competitive mastery for Engineering (IIT-JEE) and Medical (NEET) careers. Conducted inside our dedicated Air-Conditioned Campus, this program builds strong analytical reasoning, problem solving, and conceptual scientific clarity.

Climate-Controlled Comfort: Full A/C environment ensures maximum focus and stamina during study hours.
Concept Mastery: Advanced syllabus covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology beyond board level.
Speed & Accuracy Testing: Weekly objective OMR practice papers and problem-solving shortcuts.
STEM Mentorship: Taught by subject teachers specialized in competitive exam coaching.

Mental Ability & Logic: Systematic training in spatial reasoning and mental mathematics.
OMR Exam Practice: Regular simulated test series to build speed and confidence.
